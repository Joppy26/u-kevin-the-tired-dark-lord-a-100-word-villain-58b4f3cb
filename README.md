Entry for [The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb) on make.fun: a 100-word monologue for a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired. The tiredness lives in the rhythm, the pauses and the small deflections, never in a plain statement, and the speaker is still a villain with a scheme and a history.

`main.ts` deterministically writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Right. The hero has come. Just as I've finally got the kettle on and the maps spread out — maps of a kingdom I conquered forty years ago and still haven't finished the paperwork for. Sit down. No, not the throne. The chair. The one with the cushion. I keep meaning to mend the draft in the east tower, and the moat, and — ah, the moat can wait. It has waited forty years. It can wait one more Tuesday. Now. Your sword. Put it down. We will talk terms. And you, hero, may take the kettle. I am keeping the maps.

Run it with `node main.ts`. No input, no randomness, no network, so every run produces the exact same bytes.

Gate: the entry is 100 words, inside the 1–600 character limit.
